01Can I convert contract staff to permanent employees?
Yes. Conversion terms are agreed up front in your service agreement.
02What is the difference between contract staffing and permanent recruitment?
With contract staffing, TASC employs the associate and manages visas, payroll and compliance while they work for you. With permanent recruitment, we source and screen and you employ directly.
03How much do contract staffing services cost in Dubai?
Pricing depends on role, volume and contract length.
