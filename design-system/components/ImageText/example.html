In Saudi Arabia This Service is called GRO

GRO services in Saudi Arabia

The service is the same one. The term is not. In Saudi Arabia the role is a Government Relations Officer, and the work runs through a different set of platforms: Qiwa for labour contracts and establishment files, Muqeem for iqama and exit re-entry, Absher for individual government services.
