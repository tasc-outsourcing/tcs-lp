What Changes in 2027, and When

The dates we cover on the day, as announced at the time of publishing. If anything moves before 15 December, we cover the update live.

3 Jan

Tourism, phase two

New Saudization rates for tourism roles, including 30% for chefs.

14 Feb

Project management, 70%

For establishments with three or more people in project management roles.

25 Apr

Nitaqat wage threshold

A Saudi employee must earn SAR 5,000 to count in full. SAR 4,000 to 4,999 counts as half.

23 May

Financial professions, 50%

A 50% rate across 17 finance job titles. [23 May date to confirm]

Already in force Sales and marketing 60% (minimum salary SAR 5,500) · Accounting 50%, rising in steps to 70% · Procurement 70%
